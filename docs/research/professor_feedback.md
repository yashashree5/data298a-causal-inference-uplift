# Professor Feedback and Revised Direction

## Initial Direction

The initial project proposal used:
- T-Learner
- Causal Forest
- DR-Learner
- Policy Learner

The application was autonomous-driving planner experimentation and regression intelligence using nuPlan.

## Professor Feedback

During the abstract presentation, the team was advised to explore newer AI approaches instead of relying only on the originally proposed traditional causal/ML models.

The team is therefore investigating:
- Transformer architectures
- Attention-based methods
- Temporal sequence modeling
- Interaction-aware architectures
- Other recent AI approaches relevant to autonomous-driving scenarios

## Current Status

The final four project models have not yet been selected.

The team is evaluating candidate approaches based on:
1. fit with nuPlan data,
2. relevance to Topic 20,
3. implementation complexity,
4. compute requirements,
5. availability of reproducible implementations,
6. usefulness for scenario-level planner regression analysis.

## Current Decision

The AV planner experimentation use case remains under active scoping. The modeling layer will be finalized after the current literature review and feasibility investigation.
