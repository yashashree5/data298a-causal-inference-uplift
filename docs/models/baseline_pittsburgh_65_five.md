## Protocol

Evaluation is out of fold over the modeling table's log-grouped `fold` column: for each fold, every model is fitted on the other folds and predicts the held-out rows, and all metrics are computed on the pooled out-of-fold predictions. Every learned preprocessing step (median imputation, scaling, category encoding) is fitted on the training folds only. There is no separate test split; the top-25 manifest's train/val/test split is not used here.

Hyperparameters are fixed, not tuned. With about 13 rows per fold on the 65-row table, nested cross-validation would tune on about 10 rows per inner fold, so the chosen settings would follow fold noise, and tuning on the outer folds would leak evaluation rows into model selection. Ridge uses `alpha` 1.0; boosting is shallow, slow and regularized (`max_depth` 3, `learning_rate` 0.05, `max_iter` 200, `min_samples_leaf` 5, `l2_regularization` 1.0, no early stopping). The same settings are used on every table so runs stay comparable.
