## Notes

- 84 train logs come from a recording that also has a mini log; 15 of them are the identical log. All are excluded from benchmark candidates so the mini_68 results stay independent.
- Candidate anchors within one recording overlap heavily in time. Counts are an upper bound on independent scenarios; selection must still reject overlapping windows per recording.
- Most anchors are lost at the mission-goal rule because the source scenes have no goal: in a 400-log sample, 54-72% of scenes per city had a NULL `goal_ego_pose_token` and none pointed to a missing ego_pose. The simulation runs with `remove_invalid_goals=true`, so these cannot be simulated.
- After the goal rule, the roadblock and window rules remove no further anchors in this data.
- A cell of 0 means that category cannot be sampled in that city (`traversing_pickup_dropoff` exists only in Las Vegas). Small cells limit equal per-city quotas.
